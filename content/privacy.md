---
title: "Privacy"
description: "No cookies, no analytics, no tracking."
---

This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking.

The prompt keeps your command history in your browser's `sessionStorage`. It
never leaves your device and is deleted when you close the tab.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs visitors' IP addresses for
security purposes under
[GitHub's own privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
